# Premium movie channels: official viewing options

These are subscription networks, **not free IPTV streams**. Availability varies by package and region; streaming apps may offer on-demand catalogs instead of every linear channel.

| Network / channel family | Official viewing option |
| --- | --- |
| HBO, HBO2, HBO Signature, HBO Family, HBO Comedy, HBO Latino | [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/) or participating TV providers |
| Cinemax, MoreMAX, ActionMAX, ThrillerMAX, MovieMAX | [Cinemax](https://www.cinemax.com/) via participating TV providers |
| Showtime, Showtime 2, Showcase, Extreme | [Paramount+](https://www.paramountplus.com/) premium plan or participating TV providers; verify linear-channel availability |
| Starz, Starz Edge, Starz Cinema, Starz Encore | [Starz](https://www.starz.com/) or participating TV providers |
| MGM+, MGM+ Hits, MGM+ Drive-In | [MGM+](https://www.mgmplus.com/) or participating TV providers |

Official subscription providers may not expose reusable M3U URLs. Do not store credentials, cookies, DRM keys or unauthorized endpoints in this repository.
